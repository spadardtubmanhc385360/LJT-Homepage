I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am supervised by Professor Junxian He. Professor Junxian He also previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), from which I graduated in June 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
